Number formats

Weights in 8 or 4 bits halve or quarter the bytes each step reads

FormatBytes per weightRuns on the L4How
BF162YesAs trained
FP81Yes, FP8 tensor coresPer-channel scales, often without calibration data
INT4 (weight-only)0.53Yes, unpacked to 16 bitsGroup scales, calibration: AWQ, GPTQ

4-bit counts 0.53 bytes: the scales take the rest. The L4 sheet's FP8 rate is twice its BF16 rate.

Two families: weight-only formats (INT4) save memory and bandwidth but compute in 16 bits after unpacking; formats with native tensor cores (FP8 on Ada and later) also speed up the arithmetic. The T4 of a free notebook has neither FP8 nor BF16 tensor cores. Sources: vLLM quantization page; AWQ (Lin et al., 2023, arXiv 2306.00978); LLM.int8() (Dettmers et al., 2022, arXiv 2208.07339).

Speculative decoding

A small model drafts a few tokens; the base model checks them in one step

DraftQwen3-0.6B proposes k tokens
Verifythe base scores all k at once
Keepthe correct run, plus one token of its own

With greedy decoding the answer is exactly what the base model alone would have written.

Verification reuses one read of the weights for k + 1 positions, which is free while decode waits on memory (Day 1). The sampling version keeps the base model's distribution exactly. Sources: Leviathan et al., 2023 (arXiv 2211.17192); Chen et al., 2023 (arXiv 2302.01318); vLLM speculative decoding page.

Distillation

Musamir-mini is a 1.7B model trained on the base model's answers

Cheaper

1,171 output tokens a second on one L4 while meeting the objective, against 441 for the base.

Worse alone

Arabic 0.583, English 0.703: under both floors by far.

Musamir-mini is part of the case: Qwen3-1.7B distilled on the authority's traffic. Its outcomes are synthetic.

Distillation trains a small student on a large teacher's outputs (Hinton et al., 2015, arXiv 1503.02531). The student matches the teacher on what it saw a lot of and falls behind on the rest. The data team's traffic is mostly English, which is why Arabic falls further: 16 points against 13.
