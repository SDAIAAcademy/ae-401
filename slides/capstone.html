Capstone

Monday's deployment, tuned to the objective at the lowest cost per successful request

Hanadi's brief

90% of peak requests within 2.0 s to the first token, 150 ms a token and no pause over 1.0 s; Arabic at least 0.73 and English 0.81; no cache block across tenants; no personal data in a cached entry.

The evidence

A public repository: raw benchmark rows, engine logs, quality by language, the isolation test, a design document with cost and energy per request, and the commit history.

The panel plays Hanadi (the objective and the decision), Rawya (isolation and personal data) and Kenan (cost). Remind presenters that every number must come from a run in their repository, and that saying the hosted quote is cheaper is an acceptable conclusion if the evidence says so.

Full marks, briefly: cost model, a replayed day with input tokens in the quote and a break-even; benchmark method, three runs a point and all three clocks; optimisations, one change at a time with quality per language; topology, a router with a load limit and a scaling policy justified by cold start; reproducibility, a stranger reruns the repository. The details are in labs/capstone/README.md.

Format

12 minutes of talk, 8 minutes of questions

1 · The baseline

The curve of the provided deployment and the three diagnoses.

2 · What you changed

Each change with goodput and quality per language; the topology.

3 · The decision

Cost per successful request against the quote, and what the Director must accept.

In the questions. The assessor changes the traffic mix; you predict the goodput effect, then rerun one point.

The prediction before the rerun is the authenticity check, with the raw benchmark files, engine logs and commit history. How the course is assessed: the supervised entry and exit checks, AI off (20%), the capstone rubric (60%) and this live defence (20%). Passing is 70% overall and at least 60% on both the capstone rubric and the supervised check; the certificate is completion with assessed work. Peer feedback: after each talk, everyone writes one strength, one question and one suggestion.
